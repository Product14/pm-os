Spyne PM OS

One shared Claude Code setup for every Spyne PM. It holds what we have learned about how Spyne's systems and data actually behave, the skills we run every week, and the rituals that keep both current. It replaces each PM building the same setup alone.

v0.2: 62 entries live, reviewed by an agent Next: weekly planning loop Owner: Yash Sharma

Why we need it

The org already gives every Claude account the dealer basics through the us-dealer-domain skill. What it cannot know is Spyne's own systems, data and decisions. Today that knowledge lives with whoever learned it.

  1. 1

    Lessons stay with one PM

    A trap like "conversation_fact returns 12 to 17% fewer appointments than dealer_leads.meetings" (Spyne internal analysis, Sep 2026) gets learned once, then relearned by the next person who queries it.

  2. 2

    Skills travel as tarballs and pasted prompts

    Good prompts and skills reach teammates through Slack attachments. Everyone copies once, edits locally, and the copies drift. Nothing flows back.

  3. 3

    Every PM sets up tools alone

    ClickHouse users, Freshdesk keys and connectors get configured one person at a time, with the same questions asked in support channels each time.

How knowledge gets in

Sharing out is easy. Getting what each PM learns back in is the whole game, so every lesson follows the same path.

1. Capture

A session ends with a lesson worth keeping, or you write one by hand.

2. Candidate

It lands in the queue as one small file.

queue/
3. Reviewer agent

A separate agent opens the source and checks the rules. It merges, holds, or sends the entry for repair. No human step.

scripts/review-context.mjs
4. Stable

Every PM's next session reads it from the index.

context/

Nobody approves anything. A rejected entry that only needs splitting or a corrected source is rewritten by a repair agent and reviewed again. Anyone can veto a wrong merge with one command, and the reviewer learns from every veto. Entries not re-verified in 90 days get flagged.

Your week with it

Built around each PM's quarterly goals sheet. Next to ship.

  1. Drafts the update from what actually moved: Jira, launches, readouts, tickets

Rules the OS enforces

  • Nothing sends without you. Updates, release notes and dealer-facing ticket replies are drafts until you approve them.
  • Internal notes only on Freshdesk. The OS can post private notes. Public replies are blocked in code.
  • Your own credentials. Every PM uses their own ClickHouse user and Freshdesk key. Nothing is pasted into chat.
  • No commercials, no customer PII. The commit check blocks both. Goals sheets are read live and never copied.
  • Nothing waits on a person. The reviewer agent merges, holds or repairs every contribution on its own. Held items expire in 14 days.
  • Every number has a source. An entry without an owner, a source and a verified date cannot be committed.
  • Personal stays personal. Your voice and side projects stay in your own setup, under names that never clash with team skills.
