# CSM draft: testing a live Vini agent

**What we heard:** Stores that are already live want to hear exactly what their customers hear from the agent, so they can catch a wrong hours, policy or script setting and fix it themselves instead of waiting on us.

**How to do it today:**
- Go to Sales or Service > Settings > Agents and click **Talk to agent** on the agent you want to hear.
- Choose a browser call or a phone call. For outbound and sales inbound agents, enter the test customer's details first; the outbound agent will ring the phone number you enter.
- The call runs on your real live agent with your real hours, policies and script, so what you hear is what your customers hear.
- If something is wrong, change it in Settings and call again.

**Be upfront about one thing:** today these test calls are treated like normal conversations. A test can show up in your conversation reports, and a sales outbound test creates a lead in your CRM. If you run a test, tell your BDC manager so they can ignore or close that lead. We know this is not ideal and are looking at keeping test calls out of your numbers.

**What we are not doing yet:** We are not moving live stores onto the guided demo experience prospects see. We will look at it again once we know how many live stores are asking for it and how long agent errors stay live before someone catches them. If your store wants it, tell me, and I will add you to the count.
