# A live rooftop cannot test its own Vini agent without the test landing in its reports and CRM

**Author:** Sales Inbound PM · **Date:** 2026-10-22 · **Status:** Draft v2 · **Ticket:** none (PM idea) · **Area:** platform
**Flywheel stage:** lead and appointment (sales), service drive appointment (service)

## Decision
Build one guided test for the Sales and Service voice agents of live rooftops, running on the rooftop's own live agent, with test calls kept out of its conversation reports. [PM] [E1]
Text-message agents and the receptionist are not covered in this release; the receptionist is decided in the PRD. [E24] [PM]
The judge said defer on unmeasured demand; the PM overrode it because live rooftops already test today and those tests land in their reports and CRM. [PM] [E101] [E63]

## TL;DR
Today a manager who wants to hear their live agent places a real call that counts like a customer call. We will give them the guided test prospects get, on the agent their customers reach, with a report and a list of past tests. Tests stay out of conversation reports from day one; the places a test still shows (step 7) must be closed before it opens to every user. The gain is catching a wrong hours or policy answer before customers hear it, not yet measured.

## Problem
A service manager changes Saturday hours in Settings and wants to hear the agent say them before customers call. [ASSUMPTION: illustrative; no dealer has been quoted yet]
Today they click Talk to agent, fill in a short form, and a real call goes to the live agent. [E3]
That call counts in the rooftop's conversation reports like a customer conversation, and a sales outbound test also creates a real lead in the CRM. [E63] [E56]
They get no report of what the agent captured and no list of past tests. [E18]
Prospects get exactly that from the guided demo, but it is shown only before go-live and blocked for live stores. [E4] [E5]
Opened as built, the demo would break the live agent: real browser calls would fail, every customer would be offered lease and payment estimates, and test edits would change live hours and policies. [E6] [E7] [E8] [E15]
The people who feel it are the GM, BDC manager and service manager who own what the agent says, and today nothing connects a Settings change to what the customer then hears. [ASSUMPTION: inferred from who uses Settings]

**Goal:** a live rooftop hears what its customers hear, sees what the agent captured, and fixes anything wrong in Settings, without the test touching its live setup, its reports or its store line. [PM]

## Evidence
- In the last 90 days, 60 live rooftops placed 431 test calls from the console; Spyne staff testing on those rooftops are included and cannot be separated. [E101] [E100]
- No dealer has asked in their own words yet: the customer conversation and ticket searches failed, so that count is missing, not zero. [E30] [E50]
- The receptionist test already runs on a live rooftop's real agent and keeps its calls out of reports, so the approach works. [E9] [E11]

## Recommendation
1. On Sales or Service > Settings > Agents, Talk to agent opens the guided test, pointed at the rooftop's own live agent. [E1] [PM]
2. The tester picks a browser call to the inbound agent, or has the outbound agent call their own verified phone number, never a customer's. [E23] [PM]
3. The agent uses its live script, hours, policies, service menu, inventory and finance provider. It never transfers, so the store's line never rings and transfers are not tested, and any appointment it books is flagged as a test. [E21] [E15] [E96]
4. The agent's details are read-only, each with a link to edit it in Settings. The test cannot change the live setup, and the automatic hours save before a test is removed. [E21] [E15]
5. After the call the tester gets a report of what the agent captured, reworded for a live store, and finds every past test in the history. [E16] [E18]
6. Kept out from day one: the calls list, analytics and conversation reports. A test service booking skips the dealer's DMS or scheduler, and a test record is not sent to the dealer's own CRM unless the rooftop opted in. [E9] [E59] [E81] [E83]
7. Still shows, and must be closed before rollout to every user: test bookings in the overview appointment count, real staff tasks from callback requests, a lead under the tester's phone in the console lead list, real texts sent during the call, and reminders on test bookings. [E12] [E82] [E94] [E14] [E90] [E92]
8. The Spyne sales team's demo list flags rooftops already live, so nobody chases a customer as a prospect. [E21]
9. Rollout: Spyne owners first on production, then every user, with an off switch per rooftop; off means today's form. Before the first production test: one run on a test rooftop with a live DMS connection proves no booking reaches the scheduler. Before every user: step 7 closed, no real agent carrying an old demo flag, and a daily check that tests stay out of report counts. [E21] [E81] [E6] [E102] [PM]

Set per rooftop, no code change: which agents show (only those the store has live) and the tester's verified phone. [E23] [PM]
Not in this release: text-message tests, the receptionist, and rebuilding any part of the guided test that already works. [E24] [PM]

## Risks and dependencies
- If any path misses the live safeguard, the real agent is treated as a demo and real calls break at that rooftop; the per-rooftop off switch is the rollback. [E6] [E7] [E8]
- Some live rooftops may already carry an old demo flag from the prospect demo, and our reporting data cannot count them. [E102] [ASSUMPTION: some live rooftops carry an old demo mark]
- The DMS block is read in code, not run, and Xtime was not traced. [E81]
- The block on the dealer's own CRM lets a record through when it cannot link it to the call. [E84]
- Closing step 7 touches tools other teams own; no owner or date yet. [E13] [E14] [PM]
- Outbound tests ring from a shared Spyne number, and cleanup at sales go-live could remove leads on a rooftop already live on service. [E23] [E17]
