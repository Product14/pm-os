# Salespeople and service advisors cannot see which customer replies are theirs to answer

**Author:** Senior PM, Vini Sales Agents · **Date:** 2026-10-16 · **Status:** Draft v1 · **Ticket:** Freshdesk 24641 · **Area:** vini-sales
**Flywheel stage:** lead to appointment (sales); service customer to RO (service)

## Decision
Build-generalised, feature my-unanswered-queue: the PM chose to build over the judge's already-possible [PM].
No reason was recorded at the gate; the case for building is the advocate's: an engaged lead waits until someone happens to look, and nothing shows a rep which replies are theirs [E16, E17].
Asked for a one-screen command board at one rooftop; building a "My unanswered" queue for every rooftop, sales and service [PM] [E27, E37].
The judge's strongest point stays open as the first risk [E8, E25].

## TL;DR
A rep or advisor cannot open Conversations and see the replies waiting on them. We add a true "unanswered" state, an owner per conversation and a live count, for both departments. It removes cross-checking Action Items and the CRM and makes reply wait time measurable.

## Problem
The used car manager wants "to only stay in one screen rather than have to cross reference multiple" [E5].
He is adding two salespeople and already has "trouble with them following up" [E6].
On about 600 leads a month (dealer-stated, not audited), a three-screen flow is one "there's no way that these guys would do" [E10].
He keeps Conversations, Action Items and two VinSolutions tabs open [E13].
Cost: engaged leads going cold (front-end and F&I gross) and manager checking time; no figure exists [E6, Q15].
Advisors work the same pages; an unanswered service customer is an RO that never opens (absorption; inferred) [E37, E39].

## Evidence
- He asked for "[my] unanswered, click on it" [E7] and a "customers unanswered" line [E11].
- The team list filters by department, unread and flow, never by owner `conversational-ai-backend:src/customers/customers.controller.ts:16`.
- Unread is not unanswered: it flips on create, call updates and dealer email `conversational-ai-backend:src/conversation/conversation.service.ts:520`, and on AI sends `conversational-ai-backend:src/sms/sms.service.ts:753`.
- Read state is one `lastReadAt` per conversation, so a manager reading clears it for the rep `conversational-ai-backend:src/conversation/conversation.schema.ts:183`.
- Vini-created action items are assigned to SYSTEM, so the owner filter misses them `conversational-ai-backend:src/crm_integration/crm_integration.service.ts:693`.

## Recommendation
One shared capability, designed for both departments [PM]:
- **Unanswered state:** stamp last customer message and last human reply on the inbound path, cleared by a reply, not a view `conversational-ai-backend:src/sms/sms.service.ts:1924`. `isUnread` stays as is; it drives service metrics and the Customers count `conversational-ai-backend:src/service-metrics/contact-metrics.service.ts:1743`.
- **Owner filter** on the team list from the lead assignee `conversational-ai-backend:src/leads/schemas/lead.schema.ts:42`; Vini action items inherit that owner, not SYSTEM.
- **Live count** and a per-member landing view, on the Needs Attention pattern [E22].
- **Alerts** to the owner through existing notification preferences `conversational-ai-backend:src/notifications/notifications.service.ts:156`.

Per-rooftop settings: owner source (Spyne assignee, takeover claimant, later CRM salesperson or DMS advisor), missed calls [E31], alert recipients [E33].
A department with no owner source gets a team "Unanswered" view, never a guessed "mine" [ASSUMPTION: team view is an acceptable fallback for service].
Not building: the five-counter board, the seven-stage journey, removing Customers, CRM write-back [E28, E30, E32, E34]. The Customers action-item defect goes to the bug path [E9].
