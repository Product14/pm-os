# Salespeople and service staff cannot see which customers are waiting on them

**Author:** Senior PM, Vini Sales Agents · **Date:** 2026-10-16 · **Status:** Draft v2 · **Ticket:** Freshdesk 24641 · **Area:** vini-sales
**Flywheel stage:** lead to appointment (sales); service customer to RO (service)

## Decision
Build an "Unanswered" queue on Conversations, sales and service, every rooftop (size L): per person where the dealer's assignment is connected, a team view elsewhere [PM] [E43].
Why: strategic. The console is the product, so the floor must work in it, whatever the ticket count [PM] [E42].
Asked for a command board at one rooftop; building the queue for every rooftop with this job [E27, E37].

## TL;DR
Nobody can see which customers wait on them. We add an unanswered state only a human answer clears, owners from the dealer's CRM or scheduler/DMS, a count and escalation. It removes the Action Items cross-check and the hunt for who owes a reply; the CRM still closes the record.

## Problem
The used car manager wants "to only stay in one screen rather than have to cross reference multiple" [E5].
He is adding two salespeople and already has "trouble with them following up" [E6].
On about 600 leads a month (dealer-stated, not audited), a three-screen flow is one "there's no way that these guys would do" [E10].
He keeps Conversations, Action Items and two VinSolutions tabs open [E13].
Cost: manager cross-checking time and hours a customer waits at the AI-to-salesperson handoff, which nobody is paid on; neither is measured [E6, E16, Q15].
Service shares the pages; its main user and RO value are unknown [E37, E39].

## Evidence
- He asked for "[my] unanswered, click on it" [E7] and a "customers unanswered" line [E11].
- The team list filters by department, unread and flow, never by owner `conversational-ai-backend:src/customers/customers.controller.ts:16`.
- Unread is not unanswered: it flips on create, call updates and dealer email `conversational-ai-backend:src/conversation/conversation.service.ts:520`, and on AI sends `conversational-ai-backend:src/sms/sms.service.ts:753`.
- Read state is one `lastReadAt` per conversation, so a manager reading clears it for the rep `conversational-ai-backend:src/conversation/conversation.schema.ts:183`.
- Vini-created action items are assigned to SYSTEM, so the owner filter misses them `conversational-ai-backend:src/crm_integration/crm_integration.service.ts:693`.

## Recommendation
One shared capability, both departments [PM]:
- **Mine** is the CRM's assigned salesperson (sales) or the scheduler or DMS advisor (service), read CRM/DMS-first; never Spyne's round-robin pick or a takeover claimant [PM] [E43].
- **Until a rooftop's source is connected:** a team "Unanswered" view, owned by the sales or BDC manager, or the service manager or service BDC lead [PM].
- **Unanswered:** SMS and chat, a customer message after a Vini handoff or flag, or once a human is active, with no human reply since; calls, a missed call not returned (setting) [E31]; email, no human reply since. What Vini fully answers stays out.
- **Cleared by** a console reply or call, CRM activity where we read it, or one click ("Handled elsewhere", "No reply needed"), logged with actor and time. Viewing never clears it.
- **Escalation:** past N minutes in store hours, it moves to the team view and texts the named role [E6]. Owner alerts only where connected, one per episode [E33].
- **Manager view:** count and oldest wait per owner [E6]. Action items join the owner at read time, never reassigned. Per-rooftop flag, default off.
- Not building: the two boards, removing Customers, console reassignment [E28, E30, E32]; the Customers defect goes to bugs [E9].
- **Full value, CRM-first:** waiting customers become tasks on the CRM salesperson and console replies post there. Needs certified write-back per provider; planned after this read-only release [E16, E34].
