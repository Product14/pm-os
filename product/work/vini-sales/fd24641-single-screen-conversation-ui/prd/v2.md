## 1. Problem
When Vini hands a customer to a person, nobody sees that the customer now waits on a human, or on which one [E6, E16].
The used car manager wants to "only stay in one screen" and click "[my] unanswered"; adding two salespeople, he already has "trouble with them following up" [E5, E6, E7].
His floor cross-checks Conversations, Action Items and two VinSolutions tabs on about 600 leads a month (dealer-stated, not audited) [E10, E13].
Cost: customer wait at the handoff plus manager cross-checking, unmeasured; service RO value unknown [E16, E37, Q15].

**Who this is for:** sales and used car managers, salespeople, BDC; service managers, advisors, service BDC [E5, E6, E37, Q10].
**Flywheel stage:** lead to appointment; service customer to RO · **Removes:** hours to first human reply per department; moves front-end and F&I gross on engaged leads that go cold and fixed-ops gross on service customers who never become an RO, size unknown [ASSUMPTION: 2-week target, 30-day review].

## 2. Evidence
- "a dashboard that says unanswered [...] [my] unanswered, click on it" [E7]; a "customers unanswered" line on Conversations [E11].
- 1 rooftop asked, 0 others in 90 days, 2 companies with the adjacent job; built because the console is the product [E25, E26, E42].
- The team list filters by department, unread and flow, never by owner `conversational-ai-backend:src/customers/customers.controller.ts:16`.
- Unread is not unanswered: AI sends set it `conversational-ai-backend:src/sms/sms.service.ts:753`; any reader clears it for all `conversational-ai-backend:src/customers/customers.service.ts:1092`.
- The lead assignee is Spyne's round-robin, not the CRM salesperson `conversational-ai-backend:src/crm_integration/crm_integration.service.ts:267`.

## 4. Scope
**In scope**
- Unanswered episodes; team view and count per department; "Mine" for sales once the VinSolutions read connects.
- Capped escalation in department hours, gated texts, manager view, episode record and events.
- Every agent on the surface: sales inbound, outbound, STL, chat if listed; service inbound and outbound.

**Per-rooftop configuration** (defaults go to the PM, Open question 4)
- View flag per department: sales on day one; service off until confirmed. Writer switch: on.
- Owner source: none. Owner-to-member map: empty. Owner max age: 24 hours.
- Unowned-queue owner: sales/BDC manager or service manager. Manager list: the escalation recipients.
- Recipients: none until set. N: 30 in-hours minutes. Text window per recipient: 30 minutes.
- Hours: sales, team working hours; service, its own hours where stored, else no escalation.
- Attestation "reps reply only in the console": false. Aging cutoff: 24 hours. Missed calls: off.
- Action-item intent allowlist per department: sales, all except lost-lead and no-meaningful-interaction; service, set after reading service intents.

**Out of scope**
- The two boards, counters, journey stages, removing Customers [E28, E30, E32]; console reassignment [E43].
- Scheduler or DMS reads (service "Mine", booking and RO clears, advisor messages, last-closed-RO owner): per provider, separately sized [Q19].
- CRM or DMS write-back: the CRM-first full-value path [E16, E34]. The action item missing from Customers [E9]: bug path.
- Any change to `isUnread`, `lastReadAt`, `has_unread` or Needs Attention.

## 10. Rollout
- **Phase 0, dark, 2 weeks:** writer on, view flags off, backfill. Reports per department before day one: baseline wait; rooftops with median wait above N; console-reply share per rooftop; clicks per rep per day; wait-to-outcome (shows or labelled meetings set within 7 days, inside versus outside N; Spyne data, correlational). No link: the claim stays manager time removed.
- **Before day one:** the CSM briefs each rooftop below the console-reply threshold and sets recipients, hours and unowned-queue owner with each department manager.
- **Day one, sales, every rooftop:** team view and count; "Mine" at the requesting rooftop once its salesperson read is live; texts only where R6 passes.
- **Service:** per rooftop once Vini Service is confirmed [Q10]; the first is a pilot with its own baseline, the next waits for its 30-day review.
- **Success check:** 1-2 live Vini Sales rooftops with at least 80% console-sent replies and 20 episodes a week in Phase 0, picked by the PM and CSM lead before day one [ASSUMPTION: 20 episodes a week and 50% click-share trigger]. The requester is measured once its activity read is live. If median wait does not fall, service rollout and text expansion stop.
- **Weekly watch, PM:** owner click share over 50% triggers a CSM call; text failures go to Vini backend; unconfigured rooftops and unmapped owners to the CSM lead.
- **Rollback:** view flag (A32); writer switch (A33).
