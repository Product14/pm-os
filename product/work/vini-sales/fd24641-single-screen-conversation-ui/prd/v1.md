## 1. Problem
When Vini hands a customer to a person, nobody sees that the customer now waits on a human, or on which one [E6, E16].
The used car manager wants to "only stay in one screen" and click "[my] unanswered"; adding two salespeople, he already has "trouble with them following up" [E5, E6, E7].
His floor cross-checks Conversations, Action Items and two VinSolutions tabs on about 600 leads a month (dealer-stated, not audited) [E10, E13].
Cost: hours a customer waits at the AI-to-person handoff plus manager cross-checking time, unpaid and unmeasured; service RO value unknown [E16, E37, Q15].

**Who this is for:** sales and used car managers, salespeople, BDC; service managers, advisors and service BDC where Vini Service runs [E5, E6, E37, Q10].
**Flywheel stage:** lead to appointment; service customer to RO · **Removes:** hours to first human reply per department, sized after baseline [ASSUMPTION: 2-week target, 30-day review].

## 2. Evidence
- "a dashboard that says unanswered [...] [my] unanswered, click on it" [E7]; a "customers unanswered" line on Conversations [E11].
- 1 rooftop asked, 0 others in 90 days, 2 companies with the adjacent job; built because the console is the product [E25, E26, E42].
- The team list filters by department, unread and flow, never by owner `conversational-ai-backend:src/customers/customers.controller.ts:16`.
- Unread is not unanswered: AI sends set it `conversational-ai-backend:src/sms/sms.service.ts:753`; any reader clears it for all `conversational-ai-backend:src/customers/customers.service.ts:1092`.
- The lead assignee is Spyne's round-robin, not the CRM salesperson `conversational-ai-backend:src/crm_integration/crm_integration.service.ts:267`.

## 4. Scope
**In scope**
- An Unanswered episode per conversation, in new fields, opened and cleared by R1 and R2.
- Team view with live count per department; "Mine" where the owner source is connected.
- Owner of record from the CRM (sales) or scheduler/DMS (service), stored with source and sync time.
- Escalation in department hours, gated texts, manager view, lifecycle events.
- Every agent on the surface: sales inbound, outbound, STL, chat if listed; service inbound and outbound.

**Per-rooftop configuration**
- Sales flag: default on at day one. Service flag: default off until Vini Service is confirmed.
- Owner source per department: default none. CRM or scheduler user to console member map: default empty.
- Owner max age: proposed 24 hours.
- Escalation N minutes and recipients per department: no default; no escalation until set.
- "Reps reply only in the console" attestation: default false.
- Missed calls open a wait: default off.
- Action-item intents that open a wait: default all except lost-lead and no-meaningful-interaction.
- Landing view: member setting, default unchanged.

**Out of scope**
- The two boards, counters, journey stages, removing Customers [E28, E30, E32].
- Console reassignment: owners change in the CRM or scheduler [E43].
- CRM or DMS write-back (tasks, posted replies): the CRM-first full-value path, after certified write-back per provider [E16, E34].
- The action item missing from Customers [E9]: bug path.
- Any change to `isUnread`, `lastReadAt`, `has_unread` or Needs Attention.

## 8. Edge cases
| Scenario | Handling |
|---|---|
| Customer opts out | Episode closes, `clearedBy = opted_out`; excluded from the median |
| Five texts in a row | One episode from the first |
| CRM owner changes mid-episode | Next sync moves it to the new owner's Mine; `since` unchanged |
| crm-core on or off | Same `externalOwner` on local leads; the queue never relies on local `has_unread` [Q21] |

## 10. Rollout
- **Phase 0, dark, 2 weeks:** R1, R2, R8 fields and events at every rooftop, flags off; backfill; baseline per department.
- **Day one, sales, every rooftop:** team view and count. "Mine" off until an owner source connects; the requesting rooftop gets it first when the VinSolutions read is live. Texts only where R6 passes, so none at the requesting rooftop on day one.
- **Service:** per rooftop, after it is confirmed to run Vini Service and a service manager names who works the queue [Q10].
- **Success check:** the 30-day wait review runs at 1-2 rooftops whose reps reply in the console; the requesting rooftop is measured once its VinSolutions activity read is live. If median wait does not fall, service rollout and text expansion stop and the PM decides keep or flag off.
- **Watch weekly:** clears by type, click share per owner, text failures, Unmapped and Stale owners.
- **Rollback:** flag off per rooftop per department (A28); fields keep writing.
