# Overnight website leads get the same first text whether they booked, applied for credit or just asked

**Author:** Vini Sales PM · **Date:** 2026-10-16 · **Status:** Draft v2 · **Ticket:** Freshdesk 24140 · **Area:** vini-sales
**Flywheel stage:** lead to appointment; credit-app leads stay with the store until it names an F&I handoff owner (Q28)

## Decision
Build-generalised: lead type picks the STL first message from a per-rooftop template, and CRM-first write-back is planned now as the resolution [E22, E36] [PM].
Asked for three openers at one rooftop; building a per-type opener any rooftop can configure, plus write-back to the dealer's own system [E1, E32].
Ask: approve steps 0 to 2; step 3 stays planned, dated once channel (Q10) and size (Q17) are known. Size by judgment: M opener, L full value. Displaces: unknown (Q14).

## TL;DR
Every after-hours website lead gets the same opener, so a customer who booked online is pushed to book again and a credit applicant gets a sales pitch. We start on inquiries only, then let lead type pick the first text, then write outcomes back. Callbacks and hand-keying leave the BDC only at step 3; hours removed are not sized (Q15).

## Problem
- The BDC calls website leads the next business day; Ava is meant to reach the after-hours ones first [E8, E9].
- The VP Marketing wants Ava to "confirm appointments when there are appointments" and stay out of credit and PII [E5, E6].
- One shared prompt writes every STL opener, with no lead-type or appointment variable `conversational-ai-backend:src/sequence-workflows/services/sequence-task-message-generation.service.ts:193`.
- Outcomes reach the dealer only through Console and email, keyed by hand, more as STL grows [E12, E13].
- The dealer was told this is "being worked on" with no Jira; "is this done?" is unanswered [E10, E25, E31].

## Evidence
- A lead type switched off stops STL at every gate, keyed on external_type; an unset type falls to DEFAULT [E41].
- Whether a forwarded email's lead type reaches external_type is not visible in code [E39].
- Forwarded leads miss the always-allow bypass on code defaults; prod unconfirmed [E40].
- Suppression sees only Spyne's contacts, not the dealer's texts or BDC calls; STL checks opt-out, not consent [E42, E43].
- Write-back needs a configured CRM; this dealer has no API [E12].
- 3 others raised adjacent jobs, one a credit-app-first flow; 0 in tickets; write-back asked here only [E21, E22].
- SMS-only widget: existing switch, set, awaiting the dealer [E4, E11].

## Recommendation
- **Step 0, this week, no build, no reliance on lead type:** the dealer forwards inquiries only; appointment and credit-app leads wait for step 1 (Q23). Before go-live: a forwarded test lead's source and type checked (Q19); the dealer's own after-hours texts paused (Q25); the STL notification email reaches a named BDC role before open with each lead's status and any appointment time, so the BDC confirms instead of cold-calling (Q26). Dates to the dealer this week: step 0 live and the step 1 answer only.
- **Step 1:** prove lead type and appointment reach external_type (Q23) and the reply and follow-up turns (Q12).
- **Step 2:** per-type opener. Credit-app acknowledgment is literal text, no LLM; appointment confirms use date, time and store, or no specifics if one is missing. Opt-out and no-PII are platform logic. Those two types keep all follow-up off until reply and follow-up carry the type; reschedule replies become a BDC task (Q27). Tasks record type and template key; clearing a template reverts the team.
- **Step 3, full value:** read from the CRM where write-back is certified (Q29), else the forwarded ADF. One reusable channel for no-API rooftops (Q10) writing lead status, Ava-set appointment, opt-out and summary; the PRD specifies idempotency, retry, alerts and partial writes.

Per rooftop: type mapping, on/off and opener per type, credit-app held out (default) or acknowledged once the store names an owner and response time, handoff recipient, write-back channel and off switch.
Not building: Ava inferring lead type from free text, a Spyne copy of the ADF appointment (Q7), any service opener.
