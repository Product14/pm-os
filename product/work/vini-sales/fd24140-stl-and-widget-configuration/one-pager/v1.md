# Overnight website leads get the same first text whether they booked, applied for credit or just asked

**Author:** Vini Sales PM · **Date:** 2026-10-16 · **Status:** Draft v1 · **Ticket:** Freshdesk 24140 · **Area:** vini-sales
**Flywheel stage:** lead to appointment, with the handoff to F&I (credit application)

## Decision
Build-generalised: lead type picks the STL first message from a per-rooftop template, and CRM-first write-back is planned now as the resolution [E22, E36] [PM].
Asked for three openers at one rooftop; building a per-type opener any rooftop can configure, plus write-back to the dealer's own system [E1, E32].

## TL;DR
Every after-hours website lead gets the same opener, so a customer who booked online is pushed to book again and a credit applicant gets a sales pitch. We start STL on inquiries this week, then let lead type choose the first text, then write outcomes back. It removes next-morning BDC callbacks and hand-keyed outcomes, and keeps the AI out of credit.

## Problem
- The BDC calls website leads the next business day; Ava is meant to reach the after-hours ones first [E8, E9].
- The VP Marketing wants Ava to "confirm appointments when there are appointments" and stay out of credit and PII [E5, E6].
- One shared LLM prompt writes every STL opener, with no lead-type, appointment or credit-app variable `conversational-ai-backend:src/sequence-workflows/services/sequence-task-message-generation.service.ts:193`.
- Outcomes reach the dealer only through Console and email, so their software development manager keys them in by hand [E12, E13].
- The dealer was told this is "being worked on" [E10] with no Jira [E25]; the ticket ends on an unanswered "is this done?" [E31].

## Evidence
- Lead type only switches STL on or off `conversational-ai-backend:src/speed-to-lead/services/speed-to-lead-console-call.service.ts:422` [E20].
- Website leads from the SPEED_TO_LEAD source can skip per-type config `conversational-ai-backend:src/speed-to-lead/services/speed-to-lead-console-call.service.ts:406`.
- No parser helper is named for appointment, lead type or credit app (low confidence) `integrations-backend:src/email-parsing/email-parser.service.ts:315`.
- Service STL does not use this opener `conversational-ai-backend:src/sequence-workflows/services/sequence-workflow-creation.service.ts:479`.
- Write-back exists only with a configured CRM `crm-core-service:src/crm-sync/external-crm-sync.service.ts:149`; this dealer has no API [E12].
- 4 companies in 90 days: this rooftop plus 3 on calls, 0 in tickets [E21, E22].
- SMS-only widget: existing switch, already set, awaiting the dealer's go-ahead [E4, E11].

## Recommendation
- **Step 0, this week, no build:** STL on for after-hours inquiries, appointment and credit-app types off; dated plan to the CSM.
- **Step 1:** prove lead type and appointment details survive the forwarded ADF, and that reply and follow-up turns see them.
- **Step 2:** opener template per lead-type entry; no template keeps today's opener. No-PII on credit-app is platform logic.
- **Step 3, full value:** CRM-first read, plus write-back of conversation and confirmed appointment through a channel agreed with the dealer.

Per rooftop: lead-type mapping, on/off and opener per type, credit-app held out or acknowledged, write-back channel.
Not building: Ava inferring lead type from free text, a Spyne copy of the ADF appointment (Q7), any service opener.

## Risks and dependencies
- If the parser drops lead type or appointment, step 2 waits on a parser change; the CRM webhook ADF client already extracts appointments (Q18).
- The always-allow bypass may mean no template is read (Q19); two config writers must both keep the field (Q20).
- Reply and follow-up share the generic prompt; without the type they confirm, then push a new appointment (Q12).
- Write-back for a no-API dealer is a new channel and a commercial agreement, not only engineering (Q10, Q21). We write into their system and replace nothing.
- The config has no department key and the parser feeds the shared lead store (Q9, Q11).
