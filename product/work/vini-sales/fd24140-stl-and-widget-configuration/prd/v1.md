## 1. Problem
- The BDC calls website leads the next business day, so after-hours leads wait overnight. The dealer will forward only after-hours leads to Spyne [E8, E9].
- The VP Marketing wants Ava to set appointments when there are none, confirm them when there are, and stay out of credit applications and PII [E5, E6].
- STL writes every opener from one shared prompt, and lead type can only switch STL on or off `conversational-ai-backend:src/sequence-workflows/services/sequence-task-message-generation.service.ts:441` [E20, E36].
- As a result, a customer who booked online would be pushed to book again, and a credit applicant would get an AI pitch [E1, E6].
- The dealer was told this was "being worked on". No Jira exists, and the ticket ends on "is this done?" [E10, E25, E31].
- The dealer will not scale STL while staff key Ava's outcomes in by hand [E12, E13].

**Who this is for:** VP Marketing, BDC manager and agents, dealer software development manager [E5, E8, E12].
**Flywheel stage:** lead to appointment; credit apps stay with the store (Q28) [E5] · **Removes:** next-day first-contact calls and the overnight wait on inquiries, hours unsized [E8] (Q15).

## 2. Evidence
- "drive appointments when there aren't appointments and confirm appointments when there are appointments" [E5]
- "We don't currently have an API... if an appointment was made, we can load it into our system." [E12]
- Demand: this rooftop plus 3 companies on calls in 90 days, 0 in tickets; write-back asked here only [E21, E22].
- The type entry is an on/off switch on external_type, and unset types fall to DEFAULT `conversational-ai-backend:src/speed-to-lead/services/speed-to-lead-console-call.service.ts:131` [E41].
- STL off does not stop the plain cadence, which has its own excludeType and excludeSource [E41, E44].
- The queue path never sends the STL text inline: it records `sqs-stl-deferred-to-api`, and an excluded source skips the whole workflow `conversational-ai-backend:src/sequence-workflows/services/sequence-workflow-creation.service.ts:193`.
- Suppression cannot see the dealer's texts or BDC calls. Opt-out is an exact "STOP" only, with no consent check `conversational-ai-backend:src/sms/sms.service.ts:2349` [E42, E43].
- Whether the ADF lead type or appointment time reaches external_type is not visible [E39] (Q23).

## 4. Scope
**In scope (committed)**
- **Step 0, configuration only.** STL on for after-hours website inquiries at this rooftop with today's opener. First text only, plus a morning handoff to the BDC manager.
- **Step 1, proof.** UAT test leads of each type show whether lead type, appointment details and credit-app status reach the lead and the reply turn, and which creation path forwarded leads take. Output: written answers and a step 2 size.

**Step 0 go-live checks (STL stays off until all pass):** the forward excludes appointment and credit-app leads (Q33); a test lead avoids the always-allow bypass (Q19); **a forwarded test lead receives its first text, since the queue path can skip it** (Q34); the first text lands before BDC open (Q22); the dealer's own texts are paused (Q25); consent, STOP-only opt-out and the send window are accepted in writing (Q24); the notification email gives the BDC manager each lead's status, else Console (Q26); and the widget smartview-config fix is confirmed (Q4).

**Per-rooftop configuration** (existing unless marked new)
- STL type entry: isEnabled, mode (FULLDAY, SILENT_HOURS, CUSTOM_TIME, WORKING_HOURS, NON_WORKING_HOURS). Default as today. Here: inquiry on, window covers closed hours.
- Follow-up excludeSource and excludeType. Default none. Here: forwarding source excluded.
- STL silence nudge per team. Here: off.
- Notification email recipient. Here: BDC manager.
- Widget channels. Here: call off.
- New, step 2: opener template per type (default none, which keeps today's opener); credit-app held out (default) or acknowledged.
- New, step 3: write-back on or off (default off), and the channel.

**Agents affected**

**Out of scope:** write-back (a step 3 dependency, section 9) [E12]; lead-type inference from free text; a Spyne appointment copy (Q7); service openers; widget build [E4]; opt-out beyond exact STOP (step 2).

**Step 2, per-type opener: direction** (M, judgment). Gate: Q12 and Q23 from step 1, plus Q11, Q14 and Q15.
- A template field on the `speedToLeadByLeadType` entry, read in `renderPrompt` (:441). It must survive both writers, `upsertSpeedToLeadSettings` (:47) and console `upsertStlFollowUpConfig` (:2408) (Q20), and sit where the bypass cannot skip it (Q19). Blast radius: four gate callers (creation :258, orchestrator :1937, `sms-v2.service.ts:1955`, check endpoint :73) and the shared lead store; new parsed fields are optional.
- Credit-app text is literal. The confirmation fills date, time and store, and omits any that are missing. It never proposes a new time.
- A no-PII guardrail beside `isBannedStlGoal` (:633); credit tools off on credit-app reply turns if a per-lead filter exists (Q31). A credit-app reply gets a holding text and a BDC task; a reschedule gets a task, or a handoff line (Q27).
- Tasks record lead type and template key. Clearing a template reverts to the standard opener. Follow-up stays off for these types until reply and follow-up carry the type. Opt-out widens beyond STOP.
- One reminder, from Vini or the dealer, not two or none (Q32). The BDC manager checks Ava-confirmed date, time and store against the board before open. Follow-up for a type turns on only after two weeks with 0 mismatches.
- If Q26 fails, a morning list goes out at a fixed time, even with zero leads. Statuses: texted, no reply / replied / appointment set / opted out / not texted (reason) / hold call until a time.

**Step 3, CRM-first read and write-back: direction** (L, judgment). Gate: Q10, Q17, Q21, Q29 and a commercial agreement with the dealer. Read the CRM where we have access, and write back through the certified integration where one exists, otherwise one reusable no-API channel. The write carries structured status, the appointment Ava set and opt-out, with a per-rooftop off switch. A failed write is retried, then shows as "not written back" on the morning list, never dropped. Writes are idempotent per lead and appointment.

## 10. Rollout
- **Pilot:** one rooftop (the requesting store), one sales team, after-hours inquiries only. There is no new flag: the STL type entry, the exclusion and the nudge are the switches.
- **Watch:** in week one, a daily query on coverage, skip reasons, follow-up rows and texts to non-inquiries, plus the CSM spot-check and the BDC manager's slip reports. Weekly after that.
- **Pause on:** a text to an appointment or credit-app lead, a second unsolicited message, an unhonoured STOP, or an untexted test lead.
- **Rollback:** switch the entry off, or turn STL off in Vini Config. It applies from the next lead, with no deploy.
- **Dealer dates:** the step 0 live date and the step 1 answer date only [E10, E31]. Step 2 pilots here first, then with companies from E22 (Q30).
