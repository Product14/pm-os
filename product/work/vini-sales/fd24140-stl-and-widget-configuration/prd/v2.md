## 1. Problem
- The BDC calls website leads the next business day, so after-hours leads wait overnight. The dealer will forward only after-hours leads to Spyne [E8, E9].
- The VP Marketing wants Ava to set appointments when there are none, confirm them when there are, and stay out of credit applications and PII [E5, E6].
- STL writes every opener from one shared prompt, and lead type can only switch STL on or off `conversational-ai-backend:src/sequence-workflows/services/sequence-task-message-generation.service.ts:441` [E20, E36].
- As a result, a customer who booked online would be pushed to book again, and a credit applicant would get an AI pitch [E1, E6].
- The dealer was told this was "being worked on"; no Jira exists, and the ticket ends on "is this done?" [E10, E25, E31].
- The dealer will not scale STL while staff key Ava's outcomes in by hand [E12, E13].

**Who this is for:** VP Marketing, BDC manager and agents, dealer software development manager [E5, E8, E12].
**Flywheel stage:** lead to appointment; credit apps stay with the store (Q28) [E5] · **Removes:** the overnight wait for a first contact on inquiries, if Q22 clears. The BDC still calls each lead, now with its status in hand; calls on resolved leads come off only with step 3. Hours unsized (Q15) [E8].

## 2. Evidence
- "drive appointments when there aren't appointments and confirm appointments when there are appointments" [E5]
- "We don't currently have an API... if an appointment was made, we can load it into our system." [E12]
- Demand: this rooftop plus 3 companies on calls in 90 days, 0 in tickets; write-back asked here only [E21, E22].
- The type entry is an on/off switch on external_type; unset types fall to DEFAULT, and the plain cadence has its own excludeSource [E41, E44].
- The queue path never sends the STL text inline: an excluded source skips the whole workflow; otherwise it records `sqs-stl-deferred-to-api` and schedules the cadence `conversational-ai-backend:src/sequence-workflows/services/sequence-workflow-creation.service.ts:193`, `:257`.
- Team-level STL off does not stop an always-allow lead `conversational-ai-backend:src/sequence-workflows/services/sequence-workflow-creation.service.ts:760` [E40].
- Suppression cannot see the dealer's texts or BDC calls; opt-out is an exact "STOP" only, with no consent check [E42, E43].
- Whether the ADF lead type or appointment time reaches external_type is not visible [E39] (Q23).

## 4. Scope
**In scope (committed)**
- **Step 0, configuration only, behind a go/no-go.** STL on for after-hours website inquiries at this rooftop with today's opener. First text only, plus a morning handoff to the BDC manager.
- **Step 1, proof** (S; STL engineering and Integrations owners; date set by the PM). UAT test leads of each type show whether lead type, appointment details, credit-app status and submission time reach the lead and the reply turn. The CSM gets weekly after-hours leads by type and BDC minutes per callback (Q15); the PM answers Q14. Output: answers and a step 2 size.

**Step 0 go/no-go, tested first (Q34).** One forwarded UAT test lead, nudge off, source not yet excluded. Its `sequenceTasks` rows name the path:
- **API path** (`createLeadFollowUpFromLead` :395; STL task with smsSentAt): go as approved. Re-run with the source excluded: the first text still goes and no FOLLOW_UP task exists (A8).
- **Queue path** (STL SKIPPED, `sqs-stl-deferred-to-api`, cadence scheduled): do not exclude the source, since exclusion removes the whole workflow (:197 to :208). No existing setting is known that sends the first text here while holding the cadence (:193). No-go: STL off, forward not switched on, BDC works as today, PM decides (section 11). A setting found later is retested against A4 to A10.
- **Neither, or rows from both:** no-go until engineering traces it.

**Other go-live checks (all must pass):**
- Forward excludes appointment and credit-app leads, and skips customers with an open BDC-assigned lead or the dealer accepts their first text in writing (Q33). Test lead avoids the bypass (Q19).
- Source string stable across test forwards; no other feed or STL source at this team writes it or loses the nudge, or each is listed and accepted (Q36). Forward lag under 6 hours.
- Window stated in hours and timezone, inside 8 am to 9 pm customer local time, first text before BDC open (Q22).
- Dealer texts paused for leads Spyne texts, or the VP Marketing accepts that skipped leads get no overnight touch (Q25). Consent, STOP-only opt-out and window accepted in writing by the GM or dealer principal (Q24).
- STL email carries R5's fields, else the VP Marketing accepts Console in writing (Q26). The store owns reminders; if Vini also sends one, the BDC drops its own (Q32). No after-hours transfer to an empty line (Q35). Widget fix confirmed (Q4).

**Per-rooftop configuration** (existing unless marked new)
- STL type entry (isEnabled, mode): inquiry on, window per Q22. Follow-up excludeSource: forwarding source, API path only. Silence nudge: off. Email recipient: BDC manager. Widget: call off.
- New, step 2: opener template per type (default none keeps today's opener); credit-app held out (default) or acknowledged. New, step 3: write-back on or off (default off) and channel.

## 10. Rollout
- **Pilot:** one rooftop, one sales team, after-hours inquiries only, API path only. No new flag: the type entry, the exclusion and the nudge are the switches.
- **Watch:** R10; BDC manager's slip reports to the CSM.
- **Pause on:** a text to an appointment or credit-app lead, a second unsolicited message, an unhonoured opt-out, a text outside the window, or an untexted test lead.
- **Rollback:** entry off from the next lead, no deploy; for a bypass lead or a failed write, the dealer removes the forward.
- **Dealer message (CSM):** "Inquiries go live N business days after your after-hours texts are paused and the test leads pass. Whether appointment and credit-app texts can be built is answered by <step 1 date>. Write-back is planned, and we date it once the channel is agreed." Step 2 pilot order stays internal.
