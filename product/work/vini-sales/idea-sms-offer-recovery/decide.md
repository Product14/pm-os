# Decide: SMS leads who don't take the first offered times, or whose car isn't in stock, get no second path to a visit

## Ladder
1. **Literal ask:** "both sms wins" [E1]: the PM's pick of the two SMS levers the 21 Oct weekly review ranked next, in one PRD and SMS only (decisions.md, Fixed intents). Lever 1: after offered visit times are not taken, change the offer instead of repeating the same pair or going quiet [E2] [E3]. Lever 2: when the requested car is not in stock, offer a similar-unit or sourcing path that still ends in a visit time [E6]. No dealer asked for either lever in these words [E38] [E40].
2. **Job:** an engaged SMS lead leaves the first thread with a set visit, so the store's BDC does not have to work it again. Today 99 of 517 qualified SMS leads offered two clock times booked (19%, 10 to 20 Oct) [E5]; of 47 winnable threads with an untaken offer, the agent repeated the same pair in 20 and sent nothing further in 12 [E2]; 10 of 84 winnable threads ended on an inventory miss with no similar-unit or sourcing offer [E10] [E6]. In P&L terms the job is cost removed (BDC follow-up touches on leads the store already paid to acquire) and days removed (lead to visit, and so lead to sale). Neither figure exists in the evidence: BDC touches per unbooked SMS lead is unknown [Q6], and the lead-to-visit days saved by a visit set inside the first thread is an assumption, not measured [case.md Job] [Q7]. The dealer-side statement of the job is "anytime someone agrees to an appointment, it needs to be, great. Does today or tomorrow work better for you?" [E31], and a lead whose intended visit never reached the CRM was lost with "nobody would have ever known" [E33].
3. **Who else has this job:** 3 rooftops in customer calls in the last 90 days raised the same job (unbooked text leads lost, mishandled or not handed back), all inside 30 days [E40] [E30] [E33] [E34]; none named repeated pairs or silence specifically [E40], and E30 is a different failure on the same job (a yes not closed, not an untaken offer). 1 rooftop pushes the other way: move text leads to a phone call [E35] (a dead-deal campaign in Canada, not inbound SMS). Lever 2 has 0 rooftops [E40]; the only stock remark was about another vendor [E36]. Dealer tickets on either lever: 0 in 90 days [E38]; emails and Slack: 0 in 30 days [E39]. Measured, not asked: SMS ran at 21 rooftops in the week of 12 Oct [E11] and one repeated literal time appeared at 8 rooftops [E8], and in the sample the 47 untaken-offer threads span 10 of 14 rooftops, though two stores carry 27 of them; the 10 inventory dead ends span 5 rooftops, 6 at one store [E42].
4. **Smallest shared building block:** one per-thread **offer record** on the SMS Conversation, read by both the live SMS turn and the wake-up nudge, so the message after any non-booking turn must change one thing: a new pair, the constraint question, or a similar unit or sourcing request.
   - **Where it lives:** the `visitAskState` field the voice gate already writes on the Conversation (`conversational-ai-backend:src/conversation/conversation.schema.ts:58`), on the same document that carries the SMS `messages` array (`conversation.schema.ts:64`). Today it holds ask count, pushback kind and used angles, not the pairs offered (`conversational-ai-backend:src/tools/definitions/sales/sales-visit-ask-gate.tool.ts:65`), is keyed by `callId` (`sales-visit-ask-gate.tool.ts:152`) and is switched on only for voice (`conversational-ai-backend:src/agents/agents-v2.service.ts:2905`) [E14]. No offered-slot field exists anywhere [E18].
   - **What it adds:** the pairs offered and their outcome, the customer's stated constraint (the gate's `not_until` detail already models this, `sales-visit-ask-gate.tool.ts:69`), and the inventory-search outcome (`requested_vehicle_unavailable`, `sales_new/unavailable-vehicle-discovery.xml:3`) [E24].
   - **Who reads it:** the SMS channel arm, which today has no rule for the turn after an untaken offer (`sales_new/appointment-conversion.xml:131`) [E17], and the wake-up prompt, which drops the appointment rules and whose examples re-offer the same visit (`sales_new/sms-wakeup.xml:7`, `:53`) [E21]. Unlike voice, an SMS prompt is rendered per reply, so the record can be rendered into the prompt as "already offered" with no new tool; whether a hard server-side block on a repeated pair is also needed is for the PRD [E15] [E2].
   - **Lever 2's one extra piece:** a sales action-item intent for vehicle sourcing, which does not exist today (`conversational-ai-backend:src/action-items/intent/intent-code.enum.ts:19`) [E26] [E25]. Action items already carry an external-CRM sync timestamp (`conversational-ai-backend:src/action-items/action-items.controller.ts:164`), and six CRMs receive them through one push lambda per rooftop [E43] [E44] [E45]. Only DriveCentric (a customer task with `assignedAt` = due date) and PBS (a Workplan reminder with `DueDate`) carry a due time [E47] [E48]; eLead, Tekion, VinSolutions and ADF receive untimed notes [E49] [E50] [E51] [E52].
5. **What varies per store:** each as configuration on the offer record or the intent, never a branch for one rooftop.
   - How many re-offers before stopping: the existing assertiveness level, which already sets the voice visit-ask limit (`agents-v2.service.ts:2901`).
   - Which times can be offered: the store's hours through `dealership_check_hours` [E27].
   - Wake-up count and timing: per-team wake-up settings, default 10 minutes and 2 per thread [E19]; how many SMS rooftops are on defaults is unknown [Q5].
   - Whether the store offers sourcing at all (locate or dealer trade): a rooftop setting, default off, so the agent never promises a sourcing path a store does not run. Whether stores want this is unknown [Q10].
   - Where the sourcing request lands: console action item everywhere; a timed CRM task on DriveCentric and PBS today [E47] [E48]; an untimed note on eLead, Tekion, VinSolutions and ADF until those get task write-back [E49] [E50] [E51] [E52].
   - Whether the next step after a second untaken offer is a call offer instead of a third pair: a rooftop setting, for stores like E35's that want text leads moved to the phone [E35].
6. **Every agent it lands on:**
   - Sales inbound SMS: yes, the target [E2] [E6].
   - Sales STL: yes. speed_to_lead SMS renders the same root and gets the same wake-ups and pencil [E13] [E19] [E22]; the offer record is SMS-thread state, so STL and follow_up threads get it by design.
   - Sales inbound voice: yes, at risk. The rules live in fragments voice also renders (`appointment-conversion.xml`, `unavailable-vehicle-discovery.xml`, included for all channels at `sales_v2.xml:49`) [E13] [E24]. Out of scope by fixed intent; every prompt change must sit in the SMS channel arm or behind an SMS condition, and voice keeps its own gate [E14] [E12].
   - Sales chat: yes, at risk, same raw sales_v2 root (`agents-v2.service.ts:1581`) [case.md Agents affected]; same SMS-only guard applies. Wake-up and pencil are SMS-only [E22].
   - Sales outbound: voice yes through the same root [E13], same guard; outbound SMS campaigns unknown [case.md Agents affected].
   - Service inbound and outbound: no, different agent and prompt [case.md Agents affected]. The sourcing intent is Sales-only.
7. **Full value:** "offer memory plus a structured next step: the constraint question, a different pair from live hours, and for an inventory miss a sourcing request written to the dealer's CRM as a task with a visit time attached. ## Arguments
### For building (advocate)
**1. The building block.** A per-thread offer record on the SMS Conversation: pairs offered and their outcome, the stated constraint, the inventory-search outcome. The live SMS turn and the wake-up both read it. It extends `visitAskState` (`conversational-ai-backend:src/conversation/conversation.schema.ts:58`), which is voice-only today [E14], and no offered-slot field exists anywhere [E18]. Limits, hours and call-instead are rooftop settings [E27] [E35].

**2. Why now.** Wording has already failed. The "never re-offer a declined pair" rule exists [E15], yet the same pair repeats in 20 of 47 untaken offers [E2] and in 28 of 100 threads [E3]. The pattern spans 10 of 14 sampled rooftops [E42]. Three rooftops raised the job in calls [E40], and one lost a lead it never saw [E33]. P&L: fewer BDC re-touches and fewer days from lead to visit. Neither is sized [Q6] [Q7].

**3. Fit.** Spyne-side state; no DMS touched. It fixes the Vini-to-BDC handoff nobody is paid on [E33]. Lever 2a closes on units already on the lot [E24].

**4. Cheapest credible version.** Lever 1 plus 2a only: render "already offered" into the SMS arm and wake-up, no new tool, SMS-only guard [E17] [E21] [E13]. First find out why 12 threads went silent [E20]. Defer sourcing, which no dealer asked for [E40]. Proof: repeated pairs fall from 28 per 100 threads [E3].

**5. The honest weakness.** No dealer asked for this [E38]. Two stores carry 27 of the 47 threads [E42]. Extra re-offers may read as pushing [E34] [E11].

### Against building as asked (skeptic)
**Outcome: bug.** Fix the prompt defects and the silence cause. Defer the offer record and sourcing.

**The case for it**
- The intended behaviour is already specified, and the shipped text works against it. The rule "a declined pair is never offered again" exists [E15]. But the wake-up drops AppointmentConversion, and its own examples re-offer the same visit (`sms-wakeup.xml:18`, `:53`) [E21]. That is a defect to fix in two prompts, not a new capability.
- Silence (12 of 47 [E2]) happens even though a nudge fires 10 minutes after the last reply, up to 2 per thread [E19]. Five skip rules can suppress it [E20]. Find which one fired before building anything.
- The similar-unit path is already built [E24]. The dead ends [E10] are an ending-wording bug.
- Sourcing has 0 dealer voices [E40] and 0 tickets [E38], and it needs task write-back on four of six CRMs [E49]-[E52]. Defer it until 2 rooftops ask for it.
- Demand is thin and concentrated: 0 tickets [E38], and two stores carry 27 of the 47 threads [E42]. More re-offers risk the pushing dealers already object to [E34] [E35], while close per attempt is sliding without explanation [E11].

**What we lose:** the prompt rule did not hold [E15] against E2, and nothing enforces it server-side [E8]. A prompt fix may leave repeats at today's 28 of 100 [E3]. Meanwhile 4 in 5 offered leads stay unbooked [E5], and stores keep losing visits they never see [E33].

**What would change my mind:** after the fix ships, two consecutive weekly reads still show the same pair repeated in 10 or more untaken-offer threads, at 5 or more rooftops. Then build the offer record. For sourcing: 2 dealers ask for it, unprompted.
