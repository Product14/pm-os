# Engaged SMS leads who pass on the first offered times, or ask for a car the store does not have, leave the thread with no visit set

**Author:** Vini Sales PM · **Date:** 2026-10-21 · **Status:** Draft v1 · **Ticket:** none (PM idea, 21 Oct Sales Inbound weekly review) · **Area:** vini-sales
**Flywheel stage:** lead to appointment, on SMS, before the lead goes back to the BDC

## Decision
Build-generalised, in three phases: phase 1 now, phases 2 and 3 only on their triggers [E2, E42] [PM].
Why: the same time pair repeats in 20 of 47 untaken-offer threads across 10 of 14 sampled rooftops, and the written rule against it is not holding [E2, E42, E15].
No dealer asked for this. We build it for every SMS rooftop, not one store [E38, E40].
Phase 1 is mostly defect repair. If one PRD would delay it, file phase 1 as a Bug first [PM].

## TL;DR
After an untaken offer, the SMS agent repeats the same pair in 20 of 47 winnable threads, and 12 more get nothing new: a skip rule, an empty wake-up, or a closing line that is never sent. Phase 1 makes the next message change the offer, stops a side action item from cancelling the nudge, and closes every inventory miss on a visit time. Agent-set visits land a median 1.6 days from lead when set in the first thread vs 5.8 when set later, a self-selected gap, not a causal estimate; BDC touches are not sized (Q6).

## Problem
- After offered times are not taken: same pair repeated 20, nothing new 12, different times 7, constraint asked 5, of 47 winnable threads [E2].
- 4 in 5 SMS leads offered two clock times do not book: 99 of 517, 10 to 20 Oct [E5].
- On an inventory miss, 10 of 84 winnable threads end with no similar unit or sourcing offer [E6].
  - "We do not have that older Trailblazer here. I hope you find the right one soon." [E10]
- A lead booked later waits longer: a median 5.8 days from lead to visit when the agent sets it in a later conversation, vs 1.6 in the first thread. The groups self-select [E64, E65].
- Stores do pick some of these leads up: 612 with no agent-set visit got a store-set visit within 30 days, where the CRM feed is visible [E66].
- The follow-up team feels it: one store lost an aged lead whose intended visit never reached the CRM [E33].
- A sales director wants every agreed visit closed with "today or tomorrow" and nothing else [E30, E31].

## Evidence
- **Two failure surfaces.** Of the 20 repeat threads, 9 repeated only in a wake-up, 7 only in a live reply, 4 in both [E54].
- **Live replies break a rule they already render.** Line 19 forbids a repeat "even after a side question" `conversational-ai-backend:src/prompts/base-system-prompts/sales_new/appointment-conversion.xml:19`.
  - 13 of the 17 live repeats came right after a side question: address, price, photos, trade-in [E55].
- **The wake-up is told to repeat.** It drops the appointment rules, and its example re-offers times `conversational-ai-backend:src/prompts/base-system-prompts/sales_new/sms-wakeup.xml:53` [E21].
  - 5 of 16 wake-ups copied the last agent message word for word [E57].
  - 4 were deferred 11 to 25 hours and still carried last night's pair [E57].
- **Outcomes.** 0 of 9 wake-up-only threads booked, and 8 got no reply; 4 of 7 live-only threads booked. Too small to show cause [E55].
- **The 12 silent threads are not a setting.** All run on defaults, none hit the cap, no send failed [E59].
  - 4 got a wake-up that offered nothing new; 2 were word-for-word repeats, possibly a double send [E60].
  - 5 were blocked by a non-scheduling action item written about 1 minute after the customer's last text [E61].
  - The items: callback, photo, trade-in follow-up, lost lead. Only six schedule intents are exempt `conversational-ai-backend:src/sms/sms-wakeup.constants.ts:15` [E61].
  - 1 was blocked by the outcome Human Requested [E61].
  - 2 ended on a message marked concluded after "I'll check and let you know". That turn is saved but never sent `conversational-ai-backend:src/sms/sms-v2.service.ts:4078` [E62].
  - Which rule fired is inferred, not logged [E63].
- **The similar-unit search exists.** The dead end is its ending `conversational-ai-backend:src/prompts/base-system-prompts/sales_new/unavailable-vehicle-discovery.xml:40` [E24, E10].
- **No sourcing path.** No sourcing instruction or action-item intent exists [E25, E26].
- **Breadth.** Lever 1 spans 10 of 14 sampled rooftops, two stores carry 27 threads; lever 2 spans 5, one carries 6 [E42].
  - 3 rooftops raised the job in calls, 1 pushed the other way, 0 tickets in 90 days [E40, E35, E38].

## Recommendation
**Phase 1, build now, SMS only**
- Wake-up: never resend a pair already offered, including after an overnight deferral. The one nudge offers a different pair from store hours, or asks the constraint [E21, E57].
- Wake-up: never send text identical to the last agent message [E57, E60].
- Skip rule: an action item that leaves the visit offer open, such as a photo or trade-in follow-up, no longer cancels the nudge (Q15) [E61].
- Concluded turns: a "let me check" reply with a visit offer open is not concluded. The reply is sent and the wake-up stays armed [E62].
- Log the reason each wake-up is skipped, so the next read counts it [E63].
- Live SMS arm: after an untaken pair or a side question, answer, ask the constraint once, then offer a pair that fits it [E17, E7].
- Inventory miss: always end on visit times for a shown unit, or one visit invite, never a goodbye [E10, E24].
- Every rule sits in the SMS arm or the wake-up path, so voice, chat and outbound do not change [E13] [PM].
- Measure wake-ups and live replies apart, using the per-message wake-up flag [E56].

**Phase 2, on trigger: server-side offer record**
- Extend the voice-only gate state to SMS, keyed per conversation, and block a repeated pair before send. Spans the backend and the SMS lambda `conversational-ai-backend:src/conversation/conversation.schema.ts:58` [E14, E18, E58].
- Trigger: two weekly reads after phase 1 still show 10 or more repeat threads at 5 or more rooftops [E2, E42].

**Phase 3, on trigger: sourcing request with a visit time**
- New sales sourcing intent, behind a rooftop setting, default off [E26].
- Timed CRM task on DriveCentric and PBS; untimed note on eLead, Tekion, VinSolutions and ADF until task write-back is certified [E47, E48, E49, E52].
- Trigger: 2 rooftops ask, unprompted, for a locate or dealer-trade path [E40].

**Per rooftop:** re-offer limit (assertiveness level), offerable times (store hours), wake-up timing and cap, a call offer instead of a third pair (for stores like E35's), sourcing on or off.

**Not building:** counting agent-offered times in the pencil gate, which would set visits the customer never agreed to [E23, E33]. Voice, tracked in RETCONVAI-5392 [PM].

## Risks and dependencies
- Channel bleed: the fragments are shared with voice, chat and outbound, so an unguarded rule regresses them [E13, E24].
- Pushing: more re-offers can read as pushy [E34, E35], and close per attempt slid 1.73 to 1.43, unexplained [E11].
  - Pull back by lowering the re-offer limit `conversational-ai-backend:src/agents/agents-v2.service.ts:2901`.
- Double contact: a nudge on a lead a salesperson already owns. Callback, lost lead and Human Requested keep blocking it [E61].
- The days gap may not move: store-set visits after a reply already land a median 1.8 days out, at 16 of 18 rooftops [E66].
- Wording has failed twice, on live turns and in the wake-up self-check, so phase 1 may not hold [E55, E57].
- The phase 1 bet: a wake-up that changes the offer earns more replies [ASSUMPTION: a wake-up that changes the offer gets more customer replies than one that repeats it].
- Thin, concentrated demand: 0 tickets, two stores carry most threads [E38, E42].
- Phases 1 and 2 need no DMS or CRM write. Phase 3 needs certified write-back on four CRMs, a commercial dependency [E49, E50, E51, E52].
