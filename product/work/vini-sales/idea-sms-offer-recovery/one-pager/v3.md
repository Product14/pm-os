# Engaged SMS leads who pass on the offered times, or ask for a car the store lacks, leave with no visit set

**Author:** Vini Sales PM · **Date:** 2026-10-21 · **Status:** Draft v3 · **Ticket:** none (PM idea, 21 Oct weekly review) · **Area:** vini-sales
**Flywheel stage:** lead to appointment on SMS, at the handoff from Vini to the BDC

## Decision
Build-generalised in three phases: phase 1 now, phases 2 and 3 on triggers [E2, E42] [PM].
Why: 47 untaken-offer threads span 10 of 14 sampled rooftops (two carry 27); 20 repeated the same pair despite a written rule (spread of the 20: Q44) [E2, E42, E15].
No dealer asked. For every SMS rooftop whose CRM takes the visit as an appointment; VinSolutions waits (Q47) [E38, E87].
Handoff: phase 1 changes when Vini stops, not what the BDC receives (RETCONVAI-5165). The BDC manager gains; the GM or used car manager may pull it as pushy [E29, E34, E35].
**Escalated (ceo-r1-6):** Bug-first for phase 1, and sequencing against RETCONVAI-5392; size unknown (Q23) [PM].

## TL;DR
After an untaken offer the agent repeats the pair in 20 of 47 winnable threads and goes quiet in 12. Phase 1, behind a per-rooftop flag, changes the next offer, stops nudging leads the CRM feed shows booked, and ends misses on a question. It removes BDC re-touches, unsized (Q6); no days claimed.

## Problem
- After untaken times: same pair 20, nothing new 12, new times 7, constraint asked 5, of 47 [E2].
- 418 of 517 SMS leads offered two times did not book, 10 to 20 Oct [E5].
- 10 of 84 winnable threads end a miss on a goodbye: "I hope you find the right one soon." [E6, E10]
- A sales director wants a yes closed with "today or tomorrow" and nothing else [E30, E31].

## Evidence
- Repeats: 9 threads only in a wake-up, 7 only live (13 of 17 after a side question), 4 both [E54, E55].
- The live rule exists and fails `conversational-ai-backend:src/prompts/base-system-prompts/sales_new/appointment-conversion.xml:19`; the wake-up is told to repeat `conversational-ai-backend:src/prompts/base-system-prompts/sales_new/sms-wakeup.xml:53` [E21, E55].
- The 12 silent threads are skip rules: 5 action items, all at note-only CRMs; 2 concluded turns never sent; 4 empty wake-ups; 1 Human Requested [E60, E61, E62, E74].
- The meeting skip is per conversation, blind to store-set CRM visits [E77, E78].
- Visits reach the CRM once, at creation, with no rep: an appointment on DriveCentric, PBS, eLead, Tekion (26 of 42 with an id) and ADF; a note line on VinSolutions (11 rooftops); CRM unknown at 8 [E80, E81, E86, E87, E89].

## Recommendation
**Phase 1, SMS only.** Every change reads one per-rooftop flag; off means today [E69].
- Offer: the wake-up offers an unoffered pair in local hours, never last night's. Live: after a side question, answer then a new pair; after a declined pair, ask the constraint once; on a yes, "today or tomorrow" (or the next two open days) only. Every fragment that hard-codes a pair gets the SMS guard.
- Price: today's verified-price rules stand; no out-the-door, payment, rate or trade figure.
- Skips: any uncancelled meeting for the lead, or a human takeover, stops the nudge.
- Action items: callback, lost lead, trade-in and Human Requested keep blocking, as does every item on DriveCentric and on PBS with a rep. Elsewhere a photo or quote item stops blocking and the nudge acknowledges the quote. Unknown CRM: block.
- Deferral: the "let me check" line is sent; no wake-up follows.
- Miss: visit times only on a unit still listed at send time; otherwise one question.
- Log each skip reason, branch and flag state.

**Pilot:** CRM-feed rooftops that clear the "flag on" gates; Tekion after Q55; VinSolutions off (PM confirms, Q34); one ADF rooftop for the skip change. **Pull back** when close per attempt drops below the pre-period or the dealer calls re-offers pushy; the PM flips the flag on the CSM's report.

**Phase 2:** a server-side offer record if two weekly reads show the repeat rate not halved at 5 or more rooftops, with a show number in hand.
**Phase 3:** a sourcing request, default off, routed to the lead's salesperson or a store-named manager, when 2 rooftops ask unprompted.
**Not building:** agent-offered times in the pencil gate; voice (RETCONVAI-5392).
**Carried to the PRD:** fragment line list; skip matrix by intent and CRM; per-CRM landing table; meeting query; measure definitions; deferral marker; miss and concluded backstops; rep in the meeting push (Q52); call offer.
