# Engaged SMS leads who pass on the first offered times, or ask for a car the store does not have, leave the thread with no visit set

**Author:** Vini Sales PM · **Date:** 2026-10-21 · **Status:** Draft v2 · **Ticket:** none (PM idea, 21 Oct Sales Inbound weekly review) · **Area:** vini-sales
**Flywheel stage:** lead to appointment, on SMS, before the lead goes back to the BDC

## Decision
Build-generalised, in three phases: phase 1 now, phases 2 and 3 only on their triggers [E2, E42] [PM].
Why: the same time pair repeats in 20 of 47 untaken-offer threads across 10 of 14 sampled rooftops, and the written rule against it is not holding [E2, E42, E15].
No dealer asked for this. We build it for every SMS rooftop, not one store [E38, E40].
Phase 1 is mostly defect repair, justified as that, not as a sized days claim [E64, E66]. If one PRD would delay it, file phase 1 as a Bug first [PM].

## TL;DR
After an untaken offer, the SMS agent repeats the same pair in 20 of 47 winnable threads and sends nothing new in 12 more. Phase 1, behind a per-rooftop SMS flag, makes the next message change the offer, stops nudging leads a person already holds, and ends inventory misses on a next step instead of a goodbye. It removes BDC re-touches on leads the store already paid for, not yet sized (Q6); no days are claimed.

## Problem
- After offered times are not taken: same pair repeated 20, nothing new 12, different times 7, constraint asked 5, of 47 winnable threads [E2].
- 418 of 517 SMS leads offered two clock times did not book (99 booked, 19%), 10 to 20 Oct [E5].
- On an inventory miss, 10 of 84 winnable threads end on a goodbye: "I hope you find the right one soon." [E6, E10]
- A sales director wants every agreed visit closed with "today or tomorrow" and nothing else; the CSM says other stores said the same (Spyne-side) [E30, E31, E37].
- Stores pick some leads up: 612 with no agent-set visit got a store-set one within 30 days, at the 16 of 18 rooftops with a CRM feed. Who works them, and with how many touches, is unknown (Q6, Q13) [E66].

## Evidence
- **Two failure surfaces.** Of the 20 repeat threads, 9 repeated only in a wake-up, 7 only live, 4 in both [E54].
- **Live replies break a rule they render** `conversational-ai-backend:src/prompts/base-system-prompts/sales_new/appointment-conversion.xml:19`: 13 of 17 live repeats followed a side question, price and out-the-door among them [E55].
- **The wake-up is told to repeat** `conversational-ai-backend:src/prompts/base-system-prompts/sales_new/sms-wakeup.xml:53`: 5 of 16 copied the last message, 4 deferred overnight kept last night's pair [E21, E57].
- **The 12 silent threads are skip rules, not settings** [E59, E63].
  - 5 blocked by a non-scheduling action item, none at a DriveCentric or PBS rooftop, so no person held a task [E61, E74].
  - 2 ended on a "let me check" turn marked concluded, saved and never sent `conversational-ai-backend:src/sms/sms-v2.service.ts:4157` [E62].
  - 4 got a wake-up with nothing new; 1 was blocked by Human Requested [E60, E61].
- **The meeting skip is per conversation**, so it never sees a store-set CRM appointment (9 of 57,667 carry a conversation id) [E77, E78].
- **Items become a person's task on two CRMs:** every sales item on DriveCentric; on PBS a Workplan reminder, named only when a deal with a rep exists [E71, E72, E73].
- **SMS and voice share one agent per rooftop.** A per-team flag in conversationNotifications plus an SMS check has a working precedent and rolls back per rooftop [E67, E68, E69]. Assertiveness shapes the SMS prompt but moves voice with it [E70].
- **Agent-set visits reach the CRM in one push at creation, with the time and no rep;** confirms and reschedules never re-push [E79, E80, E81].

## Recommendation
**Phase 1, build now, SMS only, behind a per-rooftop flag**
- Gate: team flag AND SMS medium, on the outboundCallTransferEnabled pattern; rollback is flipping it. Pilot rooftops picked in the PRD [E69] [PM].
- Wake-up: rewrite the conflicting lines. One nudge offers an unoffered pair from store hours, or asks the constraint when no hours are set (Q18). Never the last message's text or last night's pair; sent in rooftop local hours (Q19) [E21, E57].
- Live arm: after a side question, answer, then a different pair. After a declined pair, ask the constraint once, then a fitting pair. After a yes or near-yes, a two-day ask and nothing else [E55, E7, E31].
- Price: the advertised price only, never an out-the-door, payment, rate or trade figure (Q28) [E30, E55].
- Skips: any active meeting for the lead, store-set CRM imports included, stops the nudge [E77, E78]; the live turn needs the same view [ASSUMPTION: the live SMS turn does not see a store-set appointment for the lead].
- Action items keep blocking on DriveCentric and PBS, and everywhere on callback, lost lead, trade-in and Human Requested; elsewhere a photo or quote item stops cancelling the nudge. Q15 settles the list before ship [E61, E72, E73].
- Pencil: an unblocked thread is not penciled while a non-scheduling item is open; the pencil cron ignores action items `conversational-ai-backend:src/meetings/tentative-booking-evaluator.service.ts:79` [E22] [PM].
- Concluded: change only the SMS branch of `conversational-ai-backend:src/prompts/base-system-prompts/sales_new/response-format.xml:60` so a "let me check" reply with an offer open is sent; sendOutbound is untouched [E62].
- No "I'll note that" on SMS when no task is written [E29].
- Inventory miss, SMS-guarded inside the shared fragment: visit times only for a verified unit shown or alternatives the customer agreed to see; otherwise no goodbye, no follow-up promise, one question that keeps the search open [E10, E24].
- Surfaces: inbound, speed_to_lead and follow_up SMS (aged-lead scope, Q32); render tests prove voice and chat unchanged [E22, E13].
- Log each skip reason; a daily warehouse count per rooftop, wake-ups and live replies apart, is the gate [E63, E56].
- Hand-back is unchanged (idle summary note), owned by RETCONVAI-5165 [E29] [PM].

**Phase 2, on trigger: server-side offer record**
- Extend `visitAskState` to SMS `conversational-ai-backend:src/conversation/conversation.schema.ts:58`; the PRD names the offered-times field, enforcement point and blocked-send behaviour [E14, E58].
- Trigger: two weekly reads where the per-rooftop repeat rate has not halved at 5 or more rooftops, concentrated stores excluded (Q25), or miss goodbyes and unsent turns persist; show rate not down; BDC touches sized (Q6) [E2, E42] [PM].

**Phase 3, on trigger: sourcing request with a visit time**
- Sourcing intent, rooftop setting default off, only where it lands as a person's timed task (DriveCentric; PBS with a rep on the deal) until write-back is confirmed elsewhere (Q17) [E26, E72, E73].
- A failed push must be detectable; the sync timestamp is not proven set [E53, E76]. Trigger: 2 rooftops ask, unprompted [E40].

**Per rooftop:** the flag, offerable times, wake-up timing and cap, sourcing. A call offer instead of a third pair stays off until the PRD names who places the call [E35].

**Not building:** counting agent-offered times in the pencil gate [E23]. Voice, tracked in RETCONVAI-5392 [PM].

## Risks and dependencies
- No new CRM write, but phase 1 rides the existing meeting push: VinSolutions gets a note line, no CRM gets a rep, confirmed pencils keep their first time [E80, E81, E87]. [ASSUMPTION: phase 1 pilots first at rooftops whose CRM takes the visit as an appointment, not VinSolutions]
- Adjacent, not fixed here: one store lost an aged lead whose agent-set visit never reached its CRM; cause not traced [E33].
- Channel bleed: fragments are shared with voice, chat and outbound [E13, E24, E67].
- Pushing: re-offers can read as pushy [E34, E35]; close per attempt slid 1.73 to 1.43, unexplained [E11]. Pull back with the flag [E69].
- Wording has failed twice [E55, E57]. The bet: [ASSUMPTION: a wake-up that changes the offer gets more customer replies than one that repeats it].
- No confirmation or reminder path is known for agent-set SMS visits (Q20).
- Thin, concentrated demand (Q31) [E38, E42]. Phase 3 needs task write-back on four CRMs, a provider negotiation [E49, E52].
