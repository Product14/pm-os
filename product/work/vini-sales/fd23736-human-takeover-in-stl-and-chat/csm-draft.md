Thanks for raising this, and sorry the 24 September ETA came and went without an update from us.

**What is happening**
- RETCONVAI-5165 is going into the current sprint, owned by our engineering tech lead. Target date: {date agreed with the tech lead}. Nothing new needed to be specced: the story already covers exactly what you asked for.
- From then on, every time Vini and a rep swap ownership of a text or web chat conversation, a note lands on the lead in DriveCentric. That covers four moments: a rep takes over, Vini flags the conversation for the team, a rep hands it back, and Vini picks it up again on its own after 18 hours with no rep activity. It can be switched on or off per rooftop.

**What the takeover note will say**
> "Vini texting paused. Conversation is now handled by {rep name} ({channel}). Takeover at {time, store time zone}. Calls continue as scheduled."

We changed one thing from your request. Your ticket asked for a note saying "the AI has been paused". Today a takeover stops Vini's texts but not its scheduled calls, and that is on purpose: a rep taking over the text thread does not always mean the lead should stop getting calls. So the note says "texting paused" and "calls continue as scheduled", so the floor is never told something that is not true.

**What we are not doing yet, and why**
- **Stopping Vini's calls when a rep takes over.** That would change how takeover works for every store. We will revisit it if stores tell us the calls are a problem.
- **Pausing Vini when a rep works the lead only in DriveCentric and never presses takeover in the Console.** We would need DriveCentric to let us read rep activity on the lead, and we do not have that access today. That is a conversation with DriveCentric, not a quick build.
- **When we will look again:** if 2 or more stores raise Vini texting or calling a lead a rep already owns within 60 days of the note going live, or if DriveCentric gives us read access to rep activity. Either one reopens this.

**What would help from you**
- Do the reps here press takeover in the Console, or do they work these leads only in DriveCentric? If it is only DriveCentric, the note will not fire for them, and we should talk about how they work leads before go-live.
- If the store raises a call going out on a lead a rep already owns, send it to us. It counts toward the revisit.
